# The-Calico-Z

## August 17, 2026 Update

### Where have I been

Life has been a whirlwind the last few months.  Some of you may know I'm an officer in my local chapter Z Car Club, this has proven to be more work than anticipated.  As the events coordinator I've been busy planning events, attending officer meetings and attempting to revitalize some aspects of the club.  I've also had a number of personal events going on that have impacted my free time as of late.

I did submit an article to the Z Car Club Magazine doing coverage of Texas Z Fest 2026.  That ate up a weekend but was well worth the effort.  That article should be in the upcoming Summer August issue for anyone interested.

### Update

I've not made much progress on the car lately.  I've made the decision to reuse the webbed intake manifold, which led to a bunch of problems cropping up.  I removed the EGR since I do have an EGR block off plate however I broke both mounting studs so now I need to attempt to extract them.  I may take this as an excuse to buy a budget 120v welder and try my hand at welding a nut on the ends of the broken studs to extract them.  I spent some time cleaning up the manifold as well, mostly cleaning up the manifold gasket from the mating surface.  I had already put new injectors, an aftermarket fuel rail, deleted the cold start injector and reworked some of the wiring associated with the fuel injection system years ago when I first got the car.

I'm facing a 2 week vacation in less than a week.  I intend to have the AC drained by a local mobile AC tech so I can remove all the hoses that run along the frame rails in the engine bay to prepare to paint them as they've developed some surface rust over the years.  I will be using some Eastwood rust encapsulator black over the lower part of the engine bay to clear up this rust.  I was considering trying to find factory matched paint but at this point I just want to get moving on things and not fret too much about the details.  At some point down the line this engine will be coming back out and at that point we'll be stripping the bay for actual paint work.

I'm a bit bummed to be removing the factory AC system but I know long term it's just going to be problems and hence have made the decision to install either a vintage air system or a 12v AC system into the car.  I'm leaning towards vintage air as they're local to me.  A peer on our club officer team put up his car to have it used as the development vehicle for their s30 system offering and another officer in the club (the same guy rebuilding my L28) is now an official vendor for vintage air.

Along with removing the AC I need to remove the brake master and booster to make room to prep and paint, since the brake master is leaking.  I have the Wilwood upgrade brake master and intend to either order a rebuild kit for the brake master or purchase a reman one.  The booster is a bit tougher as the 2+2 is larger than the booster used on the 2 seaters so options are limited for it.
